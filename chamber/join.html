NP Membership Benefits

Nonprofit organizations can participate in chamber planning, obtain event scholarships, and receive free listing support.

  • Volunteer event coordination support
  • Free chamber directory listing
  • Invites to community partnership meetings
  • Access to nonprofit grant workshops

Bronze Membership Benefits

Bronze members get basic exposure, networking invitations, and digital promotion from the chamber.

  • Chamber newsletter listing
  • Invitation to monthly networking events
  • Business growth resource guides
  • Access to local supplier directories

Silver Membership Benefits

Silver members receive enhanced promotion, event discounts, and training access for growing their business.

  • Featured business spotlight
  • Discounted workshop registration
  • Social media shout-outs
  • Priority event invites

Gold Membership Benefits

Gold members enjoy premium advertising, sponsorship priority, and executive-level business development support.

  • Homepage spotlight placements
  • Leadership roundtables
  • Sponsored event promotion
  • One-on-one business coaching
